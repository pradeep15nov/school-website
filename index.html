About us

Learning that feels like home

Since 1985, R. P. Memorial School has helped children discover what they love and build the habits to pursue it. Our teachers know every student by name, and our families are part of the journey.

  • Average class size of 22 students
  • Smart classrooms and modern science labs
  • Counselling and learning support for every child
“Education is not the filling of a pail, but the lighting of a fire.” Our guiding idea, from the Principal’s desk
